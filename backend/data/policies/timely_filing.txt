SYNTHETIC PAYER POLICY — TIMELY FILING (Policy ID: TF-405)
Section 1: Standard
Claims must be received within 90 days of the date of service. Claims
received after this window are denied under code CO-29 (timely filing
limit exceeded).

Section 2: Appeal Requirements
An appeal must demonstrate either (a) the claim was originally submitted
within the filing window, with proof of timely submission such as a
clearinghouse acceptance report, or (b) a qualifying exception applied
(e.g., delayed eligibility determination, retroactive enrollment, or payer
system outage). The AI assistant must not fabricate a submission date or
confirmation number — it may only cite proof-of-timely-filing evidence that
was actually supplied.

Section 3: Exceptions
Exceptions to timely filing are evaluated case by case and require
documented proof. This is synthetic demo data.
