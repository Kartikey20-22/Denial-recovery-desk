SYNTHETIC PAYER POLICY — DUPLICATE CLAIM (Policy ID: DC-620)
Section 1: Standard
A claim is denied under code CO-18 when the payer's system identifies it
as a duplicate of a previously processed claim for the same member,
provider, date of service, and procedure.

Section 2: Appeal Requirements
A duplicate-claim appeal must show the two claims are not in fact
duplicates — for example, different procedures, different dates of
service, or a corrected/replacement claim submitted per payer
instructions. The AI assistant must not invent claim numbers or dates; it
may only reference claim identifiers actually present in the case data.

Section 3: Corrected Claims
Corrected claims must be submitted with the appropriate frequency code and
reference the original claim number. This is synthetic demo data.
