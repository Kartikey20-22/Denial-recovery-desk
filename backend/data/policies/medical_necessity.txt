SYNTHETIC PAYER POLICY — MEDICAL NECESSITY (Policy ID: MN-210)
Section 1: Standard
Services are covered only when medical necessity is supported by clinical
documentation consistent with nationally recognized guidelines. Denials
under code CO-50 indicate the submitted documentation did not establish
necessity for the level of care billed.

Section 2: Appeal Requirements
A medical-necessity appeal must reference specific clinical evidence
(diagnosis, symptoms, prior treatment failures, or objective findings)
already present in the member's chart. The AI assistant must never invent
clinical facts, lab values, or diagnoses that are not present in the
supplied evidence. If supporting clinical documentation is not available,
the appeal must explicitly say documentation is missing and request it
from the treating provider before submission.

Section 3: Peer-to-Peer Review
Providers may request a peer-to-peer review with the payer's medical
director as an alternative or supplement to a written appeal. This is
synthetic demo data.
