SYNTHETIC PAYER POLICY — ELIGIBILITY (Policy ID: EL-512)
Section 1: Standard
Services rendered to a member who was not active on the plan on the date
of service are denied under code CO-27 (coverage terminated) or CO-31
(patient cannot be identified as insured).

Section 2: Appeal Requirements
An eligibility appeal must include proof of active coverage on the date of
service (e.g., an eligibility verification record, retroactive enrollment
confirmation, or corrected member ID). The AI assistant must never invent
a member ID, group number, or coverage effective date — it may only cite
eligibility evidence actually supplied in the case file.

Section 3: Retroactive Eligibility
Retroactive eligibility changes (e.g., Medicaid backdating) may support an
appeal when documented by the plan sponsor. This is synthetic demo data.
