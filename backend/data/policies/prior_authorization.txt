SYNTHETIC PAYER POLICY — PRIOR AUTHORIZATION (Policy ID: PA-100)
Section 1: Requirement
All non-emergency procedures listed in Appendix A require prior authorization
before the date of service. Claims submitted without a valid authorization
number on file will be denied under code CO-197.

Section 2: Appeal Requirements
An appeal for a missing prior-authorization denial must include:
  - The original authorization request date, if one was submitted.
  - Proof of medical necessity from the treating provider.
  - Confirmation that the service rendered matches the authorization request.
Reviewers must NOT fabricate an authorization number. If no authorization
was ever requested, the appeal must state that a retroactive authorization
is being requested and explain the clinical urgency that prevented a
pre-service request.

Section 3: Retroactive Review
Retroactive authorization may be granted for emergent services or when the
member's eligibility was not known at time of service. This is synthetic
demo data and does not reflect any real payer's policy.
